📝 Grade 7 Task List

Your personalized task list for Grade 7 mission

📖

Build Strong Study Habits

Set up a daily homework schedule
Create a schedule that includes time for homework, studying, and breaks
Organize your study space
Create a dedicated, distraction-free area for studying
Focus on core classes (Math, English, Science)
Maintain strong performance in fundamental subjects
Enroll in accelerated math if available
Aim to reach Algebra by 8th grade
🎯

Explore Your Interests

Join 2-3 different activities
Try sports teams, arts programs, clubs, or competitions
Keep notes on activities you enjoy
Document what you like and why for future reference
✍️

Read and Write for Fun

Read for 20-30 minutes daily
Read novels, magazines, articles to build vocabulary
Start keeping a journal
Practice expressing yourself through writing
💬

Talk About the Future

Discuss favorite subjects with family/teachers
Share your interests and get feedback
Visit a college campus or explore college websites
Start picturing college life
📊

Start Tracking Achievements

Create an achievement record
Keep track of grades, awards, clubs, and volunteer hours
