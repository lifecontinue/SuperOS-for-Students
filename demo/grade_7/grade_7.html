📚 Grade 7 Mission

Age ~12–13 | Building Strong Foundations

📖

Build Strong Study Habits

Establish time management, organization and a regular homework routine early. Focus on core classes (math, English, science); if available, take accelerated math (aim for Algebra by 8th grade, as students who do are more likely to go to college).

🎯

Explore Your Interests

Join a variety of activities (sports teams, arts, clubs, competitions) to discover what you enjoy. Middle school is the time to try many things – later you'll narrow to 1–2 areas to pursue in depth.

✍️

Read and Write for Fun

Read widely (novels, magazines, articles) to build vocabulary and critical thinking. Keep a journal or write stories to practice expressing yourself, which will help with high school and future essays.

💬

Talk About the Future

Discuss your favorite subjects and career ideas with family or teachers. If possible, visit a nearby college campus or explore college websites to start picturing college life.

📊

Start Tracking Achievements

Keep a simple record of your grades, awards, clubs and volunteer hours. This informal "resume" will make it easier to compile a formal profile later.

🎯 Milestone: End 7th Grade

End 7th grade with solid grades (A/B range), involvement in a few extracurriculars, and identified favorite subjects or talents (e.g. math, writing, art).
