Capstone Project

A comprehensive research project showcasing your academic excellence

Overview

A Capstone Project demonstrates your ability to identify real-world problems, conduct thorough investigation, and produce meaningful outcomes. It showcases your readiness for college-level research and critical thinking.

Through this project, you'll develop essential skills in research methodology, data analysis, academic writing, and project management. Your Capstone Project becomes a centerpiece of your college application, providing concrete evidence of your intellectual curiosity and problem-solving abilities.

Deliverables

Research Proposal

Research question, methodology, and expected outcomes

Literature Review

Analysis of existing research and academic sources

Data Analysis

Systematic data collection and interpretation

Research Paper

Documentation of research process and findings

Presentation

Visual aids for showcasing your project

Portfolio

Documentation including reflections

Key Competencies

Research Critical Thinking Writing Data Analysis Project Management Communication Problem Solving Time Management
