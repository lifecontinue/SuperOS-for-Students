Research Honor Programs
进行中
Research the honors and awards you plan to apply for. Understand their requirements, eligibility criteria, deadlines, and selection process.
Eligibility Requirements
Most honors require: Minimum GPA (usually 3.5+), specific coursework, letters of recommendation, and personal essays or statements.
Application Deadlines
Deadlines vary by program. National programs typically have deadlines in fall/winter (October-February), while local programs may have rolling deadlines.
Selection Criteria
Selection committees look for: Academic excellence, leadership qualities, community service, unique achievements, and personal character.
