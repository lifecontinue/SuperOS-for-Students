📚 Grade 8 Mission

Age ~13–14 | Planning Your High School Path

🗺️

Plan Your High School Path

Meet with your school counselor to choose 8th-grade courses that set you up for advanced high school classes. Review the high school course guide and select challenging classes (Algebra, foreign language, computer science, etc.).

🎯

Focus and Deepen

Continue exploring activities, but begin to narrow your focus. Deepen involvement in 1–2 clubs or teams you enjoy (seek small leadership roles if possible) and consider dropping activities that don't fit your interests.

🔍

Research Careers/Majors

Start learning about fields related to your interests. Try career quizzes or talk to mentors about jobs you find exciting, to help identify possible future college majors.

📚

Academic Challenge

Maintain high grades and take any available advanced classes. Read more challenging books and work on strong writing.

🤝

Volunteer and Service

Get involved in community service (food drives, library programs, animal shelters) to begin a habit of giving back.

☀️

Summer Enrichment

Plan a meaningful summer: attend an academic camp or program (e.g. STEM or arts workshop, language immersion) or do a family/community project in an area you love.

📝

Update Your Records

Keep adding any new awards, clubs, and volunteer hours to your activity log ("resume").

🎯 Milestone: End 8th Grade

End 8th grade with a clear 9–12 plan on record (courses selected for high school), strong performance in core subjects (especially algebra/language), and active participation in 2–3 extracurriculars.
