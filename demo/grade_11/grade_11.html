📚 Grade 11 Mission

Junior | Age ~16–17 | Application Preparation

📚

Academics

Take the most advanced courses available (AP, IB, or dual-enrollment) in your intended fields of study. Aim for the highest grades you can while managing the workload.

📊

PSAT/NMSQT

Take the PSAT in October (qualifying for National Merit). Use Khan Academy or other resources to target any weak areas.

✏️

SAT/ACT

Begin intensive prep now. Take a full SAT or ACT practice test, then plan which exam suits you. Schedule your first official SAT/ACT in spring and plan for one retake in fall of 12th if needed.

🎯

College List

Finalize a balanced list of colleges (reach, match, safety). Visit campuses (spring break or summer) and continue attending college fairs. Talk to admissions reps and attend "College Night" events.

📝

Application Prep

Draft your personal statement and essays over the summer. Complete a "brag sheet" with achievements to give to teachers for recommendations. Ask teachers for recommendation letters early.

🌟

Extracurricular Impact

Continue to lead and make an impact in your activities (organize an event, start a club initiative, etc.). If applicable, present your Capstone findings or final product this year.

🎯 Milestone: By Spring

By spring, aim for SAT/ACT scores in your target range (or higher), a completed college list, and polished college essays. Have a well-rounded senior-year plan, including summer internships or jobs if possible.
