📚 Grade 9 Mission

Freshman | Age ~14–15 | Starting High School Strong

📋

Academic Planning

Finalize your 4-year high school plan with your counselor. Take the most challenging classes you can handle (honors or accelerated courses in math, science, language, history, etc.).

⭐

Maintain Excellence

Keep up a strong GPA. Start a formal resume or activity sheet – record grades, test scores, honors, clubs, and community service (save examples of important projects or papers).

🎭

Extracurricular Commitment

Focus on the 1–2 activities you care about most. Take on leadership where possible (e.g. club officer, team captain) to build depth and resume strength.

🏆

Academic Competitions

Participate in contests (Math Olympiad, Science Fair, History Day, debate, etc.) to challenge yourself and enhance your profile.

🏛️

Campus Visits

If you can, take a campus tour or attend a college fair to see what colleges offer. Talk to upperclassmen or college students about what they study.

💡

Capstone Brainstorming

Begin thinking about a multi-year passion project (a capstone). Jot down ideas for a project or research based on your interests.

☀️

Summer Opportunities

Seek a summer program or internship related to your field of interest (e.g. a research program, writing workshop, or leadership camp).

🎯 Milestone: End 9th Grade

End 9th grade with a GPA around 3.5+, a growing activity resume, and initial work on a long-term project or leadership initiative.
