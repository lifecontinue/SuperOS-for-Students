📚 Grade 10 Mission

Sophomore | Age ~15–16 | Building Your Profile

💪

Challenge Yourself

Continue rigorous courses. If offered, enroll in AP or IB classes in core subjects (e.g. AP Biology, AP World History) and advanced math/science.

📝

Standardized Test Prep

Take the PSAT in October as practice. Review your scores and set goals for improvement. Learn about the SAT/ACT format and consider a prep class or study schedule.

👑

Leadership Roles

Assume bigger roles in your activities (president of a club, team captain, lead programmer, etc.). Plan your Capstone project: choose a topic, set objectives and a rough timeline for research/creation.

🔍

College Research

Start narrowing potential majors based on your interests and strengths. Attend college fairs or information sessions (in person or online) to learn about programs that match your goals.

📄

Resume Building

Keep updating your resume with new accomplishments (awards, research projects, leadership roles). Draft a personal statement outline or journal entry to ease college essay writing later.

☀️

Summer After 10th

Pursue an intensive experience: research internship, math/engineering camp, pre-college course (e.g. MITES, RSI, PROMYS for STEM), or a cultural immersion/travel experience.

🎯 Milestone: End 10th Grade

End 10th grade with a strong PSAT score (above the 90th percentile if possible), leadership in at least two activities, and clear goals for junior year tests and college search.
